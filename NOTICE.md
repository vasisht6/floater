# Floater notices

Floater is a modified personal fork of the open-source Boring Notch project.

The original project and its contributors retain their applicable copyright and
license notices. Floater-specific changes include the music-focused notch
experience, custom interaction behavior, Bluetooth connection activity, removal
of unsupported modules from the active experience, independent local updater
